# MEBT — Design specification v1.0
F1 reference: shield, rounded m-inspired mark, upward path, three amber rays. Use supplied crop as visual reference, not final vector. Wordmark MEBT / መብት. Palette: royal blue #075DEB, amber #FFB300, navy #09234F, ice #F5F8FF, white #FFFFFF. Amber accents not small text on white; verify WCAG AA. Proposed fonts Manrope headings, Inter body, Noto Sans Ethiopic. Use fallbacks.
Mobile-first, generous spacing, rounded 16–24px cards, 44px minimum touch targets, simple language, icon plus text, restrained motion with reduced-motion support. Light theme first; dark theme optional later. Proposed bottom navigation Home, Cases, Rights, Vault, Profile; Assistance and Notifications accessible from home/header. Desktop adapts to sidebar. Avoid government portal aesthetic and misleading official badges. Show checklist completion, not legal outcome probability. All screens have empty, populated and error states. Language selection visible; use real Ethiopic glyphs and label unreviewed translations. No decorative complexity that obscures tasks.
