# MEBT — Validation plan v1.0
Recruit voluntarily across relevant documentation statuses; do not request proof, real documents or identifying case details. Obtain consent; use anonymous notes. Interview before showing prototype: last administrative problem, steps taken, time/cost, alternatives, blockers, frequency, who pays and payment access. Then ask testers to find rights, start a fictional case, record blocker, find next action and assistance. Observe unaided completion, time, errors and comprehension. Ask what they would replace and why; test pricing with hypothetical offers or nonbinding commitments, no money collection. Record anonymous participant ID, segment if volunteered, pain, workaround, task result, price response, payment preference, objections. Suggested targets: 30 interviews, 20 tests, 80% core completion, 70% preference, 10 credible paid commitments; not statistically representative or achieved. Review results by segment, fix critical issues, decide iterate/pivot/stop or proceed. Do not claim demand based on compliments.
